THE STUDY
The one idea

Every moving thing should be a tesela running, not a picture of one. A tesela is a 5×5 square holding nine independent roles. Fill the nine and the other sixteen places follow, because the square reads the same across both diagonals and through a half-turn. The nine roles are the whole state, the symmetries are the laws, and the rim is where water passes to the next tile. How God speaks language into reality, shown as water: a Word comes down from above, wells up at the Eye, runs the rings and returns as rain.

The tesela as an object

Nine numbers store twenty-five places. Role 9 is the pivot (1 place). Roles 7 and 3 and 8 and 4 make the inner ring and the rim cross; 6 and 1 and 5 are the corners; 2 and 4 are the frame. 1 pivot + 8 inner + 16 rim = 25. The quarter-turn is not a symmetry of one tile. It lives around tile corners, where four tiles meet, which is why a tile and its quarter-turned twin are different words.

The fern rule

A square of side n = 2k−1 has k² roles: 5 gives 9, 9 gives 25, 13 gives 49, 17 gives 81. One function, orbitLabels(n), serves every grid. A role cell opens into a child tesela whose pivot carries that cell's value, and the child fills centre-out by the same rule. Zooming in is a descent, the same direction the water runs.

The water

Five stations, one per ring: Ayn (the Eye, welling up at the pivot), Rill, Stream, River, Sea, then the Return as mist, cloud and rain. The loop moves only what is already inside the tile. The Word is the one thing that enters from outside it, so the engine has exactly one input that nothing inside it produces. A thought is one write to one role; symmetry expands it to every place of that role at once; the relief glides and the water carries the change outward over seconds.

The ten rules

1 State is nine numbers. 2 Height is the Bernstein surface over the net. 3 Water is a depth field moving by level difference. 4 A point gives away at most 20% per step. 5 Flux never makes water. 6 Everything glides, critically damped. 7 Seams are mirrors. 8 The cycle is closed for water and open for the Word. 9 Gravity has one direction per world. 10 The quarter-turn is a symmetry around tile corners only. The step is fixed at 1/60 s, and the same writes always give the same state.

One engine, different rivers

ANA BETA is a pit-and-peak word: S corners high, B corners low, alternating like a checkerboard, so water runs from S to B across the mirrored plane. SATOR has no pit on its corners and its lowest point sits inside the tile, so water gathers in a basin. The same engine reads the Tape, the Engine door (Ana Beta), the Bench (Sator) and the Case (the Golden Record, 9×9, 25 roles).

The tape

Only the writes are stored: which role, what value, when. The picture is replayed from them on the fixed step. Nothing recorded is deleted, and the reversal test is a replay that must land on the same nine numbers.
