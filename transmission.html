Ganymede Transmission Core

A picture becomes a sound, and a sound becomes a picture. Play the sound from any speaker, screen, video or radio, and any microphone in range can listen and rebuild the image.

Send

Sample picture loaded. Press Transmit.

Receive

Press Listen, then play a transmission near the microphone.

Test without a microphone

Sends the sample picture through a simulated room (quiet speaker, echo and hiss) and rebuilds it, so you can see what the receiver can do.
